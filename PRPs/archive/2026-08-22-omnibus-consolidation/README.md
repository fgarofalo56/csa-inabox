## Why they were consolidated

Twenty-two parallel PRPs, several of them registers claiming authority over the same
work, meant no single answer to "what is open?" Three of them (`finishline`,
`OPEN-REGISTER-2026-07-12`, `program-master-2026-08-18`) were explicitly registers, and
they disagreed. The omnibus program replaces all of them with one intake authority whose
lanes are cut by **file ownership**, so the work can actually be fanned out to concurrent
agents without two of them colliding on the same file.
