# Global schedule: what runs together, across programs

Generated by `python tools/prp/gen_plan.py`. Do not hand-edit; change the PRP frontmatter and regenerate.

Rules: at most **4** PRPs per round (WIP limit), at most **one** bicep PRP per round (the bicep slot, [`INTERLOCK.md`](INTERLOCK.md) section 1), and a PRP enters a round only when every `depends_on` and `Cross-program order` PRP is in an earlier round. A round is an ordering, not a calendar: start the next round's PRP in a lane as soon as that lane's PRP merges and its dependencies are met.

What this does NOT establish: that two PRPs in a round own disjoint files. That is checked from the `Owns` sections. Per-program wave numbers are local to a program and are shown only for reference.

After every round: `make validate` and `python -S -E ~/.claude/kits/orchestrated-prp/prp_index.py check --root .` must both exit 0 on `main`. Done means deployed and verified live; until then report merged, not deployed.
