# Azure Government service-availability and audit-scope matrix — health data platform

Retrieved 2026-10-22. Every cell cites a fetched primary source (numbered notes below). Cells marked **UNVERIFIED** could not be confirmed from a fetched page; each says how to verify.

## How to read this

- **Audit-scope columns** (FedRAMP High, IL4, IL5WI, DoD-region IL5) come from Microsoft's own "services by audit scope" tables [1][2]. A tick means the service is *inside Microsoft's authorization boundary*. It does **not** mean a customer solution built on it is authorized — see "Boundary statements" below.
- **"Available in US Gov regions"** is only marked **yes** where a fetched Learn/Microsoft page says so explicitly (region table, endpoint table, or service-specific Gov page). Audit-scope presence is used as corroboration, not as proof of regional GA. The authoritative source for regional GA/preview is the interactive *Products available by region* page [4], which **could not be fetched** (client-side rendered; the static HTML contains no table). Verify any UNVERIFIED cell there with filter `regions=usgov-virginia,usgov-arizona,usgov-texas,us-dod-central,us-dod-east`.
- **"Available in DoD regions"**: no per-service regional source was fetchable. The only fetched DoD-region signal is the **US DoD regions IL5 audit scope** list [2]. A tick there means "in DoD-region IL5 scope" and is treated as strong evidence of availability; anything else is UNVERIFIED.
- **IL5 notes** column quotes the isolation requirement from [3]. Services with IL5WI PA that *need no extra configuration* are deliberately absent from [3] ("Services with IL5 PA that do not require any extra configuration options are not mentioned in this article"). In US DoD regions no extra configuration is needed for any IL5-scoped service [2][3].

## Boundary statements (quoted)

- Azure Government regions and authorizations: "Azure Government maintains the following authorizations that pertain to Azure Government regions US Gov Arizona, US Gov Texas, and US Gov Virginia (US Gov regions): FedRAMP High P-ATO issued by the JAB; DoD IL2 PA; DoD IL4 PA; DoD IL5 PA issued by DISA." [1]
- IL5 in US Gov regions is **Workload Isolation**: "DoD IL5WI = DoD SRG Impact Level 5 Workload Isolation (IL5WI) Provisional Authorization (PA) in Azure Government … services require extra configuration to meet DoD IL5 compute and storage isolation requirements." [1]
- DoD regions: "US DoD regions are reserved for exclusive DoD use. Therefore, no extra configuration is needed in US DoD regions when deploying Azure services intended for IL5 workloads." "Separate DoD IL5 PAs are in place for US Gov regions vs. US DoD regions." US Gov regions: 150 IL5 PA services; US DoD regions: 60. Microsoft's recommendation: "Choose US Gov regions for your new deployments." [2]
- Customer responsibility: "You are responsible for designing and deploying your applications to meet DoD IL5 compliance requirements." [3] The audit-scope tables list services "included in audit scope and has been authorized" [1] — inclusion of a service in Microsoft's boundary does not authorize a customer system; the customer system needs its own ATO built on Microsoft's P-ATO/PA.
- IL5 isolation principles [3]: compute — "all IL5 virtual machines and virtual machine scale sets should be isolated … via Azure Dedicated Host or isolated virtual machines"; storage — "DISA approved logical separation of IL5 from other data via cryptographic means … keys … maintained in Azure Key Vault and stored in FIPS 140 validated HSMs … customer-managed keys (CMK)."
- Physical/network model: "US DoD regions are physically separated Azure Government regions reserved for exclusive use by the DoD. They reside on the same isolated network as … US Gov regions and use the same identity model. Both the network and identity model are separate from Azure commercial." [2]
