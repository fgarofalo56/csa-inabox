### A — practical summary for a bundling decision
- Ship freely (with notice where required): LOINC, UCUM, FHIR/THO (CC0), HGNC (CC0), NCIt (CC BY), ICD-10-CM/PCS, HCPCS II, CVX/MVX, NDC, MED-RT (VA rows), RxNorm Prescribable subset, Omaha A/E terms, LOINC Ontology.
- Ship only with a vendor licence/agreement: SNOMED CT (Affiliate licence + sub-licence bookkeeping; per-territory fees outside Member countries), NUCC taxonomy (licence request form), CPT (**never without AMA distributor licence**), ATC/DDD (**commercial distribution prohibited** without separate agreement).
- Requires each consuming organisation to hold a UMLS licence: VSAC API access; full RxNorm; SNOMED CT US Edition downloads.
- Unresolved: ISO 3166 full-list redistribution (use iso-codes/LGPL dataset or OBP reference).
